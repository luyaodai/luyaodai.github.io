Research

In the Larman Lab at Johns Hopkins, I am developing a multiplexed, high-throughput platform that profiles antibody responses: which proteins a person's antibodies recognize, measured at scale using cell-surface expressed protein libraries and single-cell sequencing. Tools like these can help reveal what drives autoimmune disease and how people respond to infection and vaccines.

Day to day, that means designing experiments, building analysis pipelines in R, and judging whether noisy biological data actually supports a conclusion. That last skill is the one I bring to evaluating clinical results.
